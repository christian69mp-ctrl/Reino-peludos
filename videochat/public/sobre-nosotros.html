What La Ruleta Caliente is

La Ruleta Caliente is a random one-to-one video chat for adults. You press “Start” and meet someone new, live, by video, audio and text. If it clicks, you keep talking; if not, you press “Next” and meet someone else.

Why we made it

We wanted a place to meet new people live where you can relax: where everyone is treated with respect, where you feel safe and where your privacy is looked after. A place where adults decide freely what they show and how far they want to go, without mockery, without harassment and without worrying about being recorded.

Our principles

  • Respect. Zero tolerance for harassment, mockery, insults, homophobia and transphobia. Women, men, trans people and couples are equally welcome. You can read everything in our Community rules.
  • Privacy. We do not record anything. Video is end-to-end encrypted and passes through our server so that nobody sees your IP address. The video carries an invisible watermark, so if someone records, we know which account did it. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. More details in Privacy & cookies.
  • Safety. Only adults aged 18 and over. Reports are reviewed by people, not just machines. When needed, we check age with Veriff, a company that specialises in identity verification.
  • International. The website is available in six languages (Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, German and Italian), and the chat translates messages automatically so you can talk to anyone.

Who we are

La Ruleta Caliente is a small independent project born in Spain. We are not a big corporation: we build and look after the site ourselves, and we read what you tell us.

Contact us

Do you have a question, a suggestion or a problem? Write to us through the contact page. You can also find quick answers in the Help centre.
