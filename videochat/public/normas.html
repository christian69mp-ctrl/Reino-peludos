Community rules

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This is a translation of the Spanish original. If there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

La Ruleta Caliente is a place for adults to meet people and have a good time. So that everyone feels comfortable, these rules are mandatory. If you break them, we may suspend or ban your account.

What we do not tolerate

  • Mockery and insults. Do not laugh at anyone’s body, looks, voice, origin or personality. If you are not interested, press “Next”.
  • Harassment. Insisting when someone does not want to, threatening, intimidating, repeatedly asking for personal data or trying to locate someone outside the site.
  • Homophobia, transphobia and hate. No contempt for sexual orientation, gender identity, race, religion, disability or origin. Trans people and couples are as welcome as anyone else.
  • Minors. It is strictly forbidden for anyone under 18 to take part or appear on camera, as is any content that sexualises minors. We report it to the authorities.
  • Recording or sharing. Do not record, take screenshots of or share other people without their permission. The video carries an invisible watermark with your account code: if you record and share someone, we will know which account did it, we will ban you and the victim can report you to the police.
  • Illegal or non-consensual content. Violence, non-consensual acts, animals, drugs or any illegal activity.
  • Spam and scams. Advertising, links, sales, or asking for money or payments outside the site.
  • Lying about yourself. Say honestly whether you are a woman, a man, trans or a couple.

Consent above all

Everyone decides what they show and how far they want to go. If someone does not want something, that is respected. You can always press “Next” or “Stop”.

How to report

During a conversation, press the Report button (the flag at the top right of the video) and choose the reason: mockery or insults, harassment, recording or screenshots without permission, homophobia or transphobia, minor or illegal content, or another reason. When you report:

  • The conversation ends immediately and you will never be matched again.
  • We keep the latest chat messages as evidence to review the report.
  • Our team reviews reports. Reports about minors or illegal content have priority.

You can also write to us at soporte@laruletacaliente.com.

Consequences

  • If several different people report you in a short time, your account is automatically suspended for 24 hours until we review it.
  • Depending on how serious it is, we may suspend your account for days or ban it permanently, with no refund of Premium.
  • False reports or reports made to annoy others are also a violation.
