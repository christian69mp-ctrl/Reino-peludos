Data controller

What data we process and why

  • Account: email, password (stored encrypted), whether you are a woman, a man, trans or a couple, sign-up date and sign-up IP. To give you access and match you.
  • Sign in with Google, Apple or Facebook: if you use it, we only receive your email and an identifier of that account, to link it to yours. We do not receive your contacts, photos or posts.
  • Date of birth and approximate location: you give your date of birth when signing up; nobody sees it and it is used to confirm you are an adult and for the Premium age filter. When you connect, we estimate your country and area from your IP address (with the DB-IP database) for the Premium “My country” and “Near me” filters; we do not store it. The other person sees your country’s flag.
  • Age verification: when signing up you confirm that you are 18 or over. Only if someone reports that you might be a minor, or our team asks, is it carried out by Veriff (ID document and selfie). We only keep whether your age is verified and when; we do not keep your document or your date of birth. Veriff processes that data under its own privacy policy.
  • Password recovery: we use your email to send you the link to create a new password.
  • Premium: status and dates of your subscription. Payment data is processed by Segpay, not by us.
  • Image protection: we show your account code (a pseudonym, not your email) as an invisible watermark on your screen and count detected capture attempts, to prevent and pursue recordings made without consent.
  • Reports: who reports, whom, the reason, the comment and the latest text messages of that conversation. To moderate and protect the community.
  • Video and audio: encrypted end to end between the two people. They may pass through our server so that neither person can see the other’s IP address, but we cannot see them and we do not record or store them.
  • Text messages: we do not store them, except the latest ones of a reported conversation.
  • History and friends: who you talked to, when, for how long and how many times (no images or messages), plus your friends, requests and blocked people, and the name you choose for the chat. So you can see who you talked to and call your friends. Your history is visible only to you; your friends see whether you are online.

Legal basis

Performance of the contract (your account and Premium), compliance with the legal obligation to prevent access by minors (age verification), our legitimate interest in keeping the community safe (moderation) and compliance with legal obligations.

How long

For as long as you have the account, with these limits: messages kept as evidence of a report are deleted 180 days after it is resolved, and the sign-up IP after 90 days. Your chat history is deleted after 30 days, and unanswered friend requests too. If you delete the account, we erase your data; payments are kept without personal data for tax purposes and, if the account was banned, we keep only an encrypted code of your email to prevent you from signing up again. Security backups rotate automatically, so deleted data disappears from them within 14 days at most.

Who we share it with

With Segpay to process payments, with Veriff to verify age, with our email provider, with the translation service (only the text of the messages you ask to translate), with our hosting provider and with the authorities when the law requires it (for example, in cases involving minors).

Your rights

You can download your data and delete your account yourself from “My account”. You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to soporte@laruletacaliente.com. You can also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or the data protection authority of your country.

Cookies and storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We only store in your browser what is essential for the site to work: your logged-in session, your language and that you have confirmed you are an adult. The font is served from our own server.

Security

The connection to the site is encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored encrypted, and video and audio travel end-to-end encrypted between the two people. The site only loads its own code and includes no third-party trackers.

Per quanto tempo

Finché hai l’account, con questi limiti: i messaggi conservati come prova di una segnalazione vengono cancellati 180 giorni dopo la sua chiusura e l’IP di registrazione dopo 90 giorni. Se elimini l’account, cancelliamo i tuoi dati; i pagamenti si conservano senza dati personali per obblighi fiscali e, se l’account era stato espulso, conserviamo solo un codice cifrato della tua email per impedire una nuova registrazione.
