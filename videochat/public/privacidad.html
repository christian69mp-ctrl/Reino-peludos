What data we process and why

  • Account: email, password (stored encrypted), whether you are a woman, a man, trans or a couple, sign-up date and sign-up IP. To give you access and match you.
  • Sign in with Google, Apple or Facebook: if you use it, we only receive your email and an identifier of that account, to link it to yours. We do not receive your contacts, photos or posts.
  • Date of birth and approximate location: you give your date of birth when signing up. Nobody sees the date itself: the other person only sees your age. It is used to confirm you are an adult, to show your age and for the Premium age filter. When you connect, we estimate your country and area from your IP address (with the DB-IP database) for the Premium “My country” and “Near me” filters; we do not store it. The other person sees your country’s flag.
  • Age verification: when signing up you confirm that you are 18 or over. Only if someone reports that you might be a minor, or our team asks, is it carried out by Veriff (ID document and selfie). We only keep whether your age is verified and when; we do not keep your document or your date of birth. Veriff processes that data under its own privacy policy.
  • Password recovery: we use your email to send you the link to create a new password.
  • Premium: status and dates of your subscription. Payment data is processed by Segpay, not by us.
  • Image protection: we show your account code (a pseudonym, not your email) as an invisible watermark on your screen and count detected capture attempts, to prevent and pursue recordings made without consent.
  • Reports: who reports, whom, the reason, the comment and the latest text messages of that conversation. To moderate and protect the community.
  • Video and audio: encrypted end to end between the two people. They may pass through our server so that neither person can see the other’s IP address, but we cannot see them and we do not record or store them.
  • Text messages: we do not store them, except the latest ones of a reported conversation.
  • History and friends: who you talked to, when, for how long and how many times (no images or messages), plus your friends, requests and blocked people, and the name you choose for the chat. So you can see who you talked to and call your friends. Your history is visible only to you; your friends see whether you are online.
  • Contact form: your name (optional), email, category and message, and your account if you are logged in. We store it as a ticket and answer you by email. Your IP address is only used to limit the number of messages; it is not stored with the ticket.
  • Notices of illegal content: if you use the notice form (art. 16 of the Digital Services Act), the category, your explanation, the URL or account code, your name and email (optional only for notices of child sexual abuse material) and your statement of good faith. We send you an acknowledgment and our decision by email.
  • Restrictions and appeals: if we restrict an account, we store the reason with that account (statement of reasons, art. 17 of the Digital Services Act). If you appeal a sanction (art. 20), we store your account, the text of the appeal and the decision.
  • Push notifications: only if you turn them on and your browser asks you for permission. We store the subscription your browser gives us (the address of your browser vendor’s push service, such as Google, Mozilla or Apple, plus encryption keys), linked to your account, and your preferences (calls, friend requests, friends online). To notify you of these things when the site is closed.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: daily counts of page views, pages, languages, device type (mobile or desktop), country (estimated from the IP with a database on our own server; the IP is not stored), sign-ups by method, conversations and their duration, and active users. Unique visitors are counted with a hash of the IP, the browser and a random value that changes every day and is discarded, so nobody can be identified or followed from one day to the next. No cookies are used. To understand and improve how the site is used.
  • Account settings: you can change your password, change your email (we send a confirmation link to the new address), see the people you have blocked and unblock them, and choose whether your camera is shown mirrored; that preference is saved in your account.

Legal basis

Performance of the contract (your account and Premium), compliance with the legal obligation to prevent access by minors (age verification), our legitimate interest in keeping the community safe (moderation) and compliance with legal obligations. In addition: the legal obligations of the Digital Services Act (notices of illegal content, statements of reasons and appeals); your consent for push notifications, which you can withdraw at any time in “My account → Notifications” or in your browser; and our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used through anonymous statistics.

How long

For as long as you have the account, with these limits: messages kept as evidence of a report are deleted 180 days after it is resolved, and the sign-up IP after 90 days. Your chat history is deleted after 30 days, and unanswered friend requests too. If you delete the account, we erase your data; payments are kept without personal data for tax purposes and, if the account was banned, we keep only an encrypted code of your email to prevent you from signing up again. Security backups rotate automatically, so deleted data disappears from them within 14 days at most. Contact tickets, notices of illegal content and appeals are deleted 2 years after they are closed. The push subscription is deleted when you turn notifications off, when your browser tells us it has expired, or when you delete the account.

Who we share it with

With Segpay to process payments, with Veriff to verify age, with our email provider, with the translation service (only the text of the messages you ask to translate), with our hosting provider and with the authorities when the law requires it (for example, in cases involving minors). If you turn on push notifications, their content passes encrypted through your browser vendor’s push service (for example Google, Mozilla or Apple).

Your rights

You can download your data and delete your account yourself from “My account”. You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to soporte@laruletacaliente.com. You can also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or the data protection authority of your country.

Cookies and storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We only store in your browser what is essential for the site to work: your logged-in session, your language and that you have confirmed you are an adult. The font is served from our own server. If you turn on notifications, your browser also keeps the push subscription. The usage statistics do not use cookies.

Security

The connection to the site is encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored encrypted, and video and audio travel end-to-end encrypted between the two people. The site only loads its own code and includes no third-party trackers. Moderator and administrator actions are recorded in an audit log (the last 500 entries: action, time and administrator IP).
