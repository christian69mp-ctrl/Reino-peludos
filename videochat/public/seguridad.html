Safety tips

Last updated: 18 October 2026

🌶️ Guindilla here! Having fun and looking after yourself go hand in hand. These tips aren’t here to scare you: they’re the little things that make a big difference, so you can enjoy yourself with peace of mind.

🕵️ Protect your identity

  • You don’t have to show your face if you don’t want to.
  • Don’t share your full name, address, workplace, social media or phone number.
  • Check what can be seen behind you: documents, letters with your address, a window that shows your street, family photos.
  • Use your chat name, not your real name.
  • 🕶️ Incognito hides your age and country for 1 hour.

📹 Sextortion and blackmail

We protect the video with a faint watermark and blur it if someone leaves the window, but anyone can record their screen with another device. The golden rule: never do anything on camera that you wouldn’t want to see recorded.

Warning signs:

  • They want you to move to another app very quickly.
  • They insist on getting your social media or phone number.
  • They threaten to publish a video or photos of you unless you pay.

If it happens to you:

  1. Don’t pay. Paying rarely ends it: they usually ask for more.
  2. Stop talking to them and don’t reply to any more messages.
  3. Keep evidence: screenshots of the messages, usernames, profile links and dates.
  4. Report them in the chat with the Report button and to us through Report content.
  5. Go to the police. In Spain, the Policía Nacional or the Guardia Civil; anywhere else, your local police.
  6. If they have intimate images of you, use StopNCII.org: it helps stop them being shared on major platforms, and your images never leave your device.

💛 It’s not your fault. The person blackmailing you is the one committing a crime. Asking for help is the right thing to do.

🎣 Scams

  • We will never ask for your password or for a payment by chat.
  • Premium is only paid in the official checkout, from the Premium button on the site.
  • Ignore links and anyone selling things or asking for money, gift cards or crypto.

🔞 Minors

La Ruleta Caliente is for adults 18+ only. If you think someone is a minor, end the chat and report them with the reason “Looks under 18”: those reports are reviewed urgently.

🧰 Your tools

  • ⏭️ Next: not comfortable? Move on instantly. You don’t owe anyone an explanation.
  • 🚫 Block: you’ll never be matched again and they can’t call you.
  • 🚩 Report: one click, and our team reviews it.
  • 🤔 The private rating “Would you talk to that person again?” helps us match you better. Nobody else sees it.
  • 🔒 Privacy settings: hide the compliments you receive and hide yourself from the ranking.
  • 🌶️ Turn off spicy mode whenever you like.
  • 🗑️ Delete your account and your data at any time from My account.

🤝 Respect

Consent applies on camera too. What someone does or shows is always their choice: don’t pressure anyone, and if the other person isn’t comfortable, stop. A “no” or a “pass” ends the topic.

📬 Help and contact

  • Help center: answers to frequent questions, and me, ready to help.
  • Report content: for content that should be removed.
  • Community rules and Privacy.
  • Contact: write to our team.

If you’re in immediate danger, call 112 (Europe) or your local emergency number.
