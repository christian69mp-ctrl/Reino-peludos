Report illegal content

Anyone can tell us about illegal content on La Ruleta Caliente, even without an account. A person reviews every notice and we will tell you what we decided.

If someone is in immediate danger, call the emergency number 112 first.

If you are in a video chat right now, it is faster to use the Report button above the video.

Name and email are required, except for content involving minors, which you can report anonymously.
