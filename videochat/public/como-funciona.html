How it works

Last updated: 21 October 2026

🌶️ Hi, I’m Guindilla! I’ll show you everything here: how to get started, what you can do during a video chat, the games, the chilis, the superpowers and what Premium adds. Ready? Let’s go!

🚀 Start for free in 3 steps

  1. Come in without signing up: tell us whether you’re a woman, a man, trans or a couple, enter your date of birth and confirm you’re 18 or older. No email or password needed. If you want, you can create an account later with your email or Google to keep your progress on any device (you need one to pay for Premium).
  2. Press Start and allow the camera and microphone when your browser asks.
  3. You’re connected instantly with someone online. No chemistry? Press Next (or the Esc key) to meet someone new. Need a break? Press Stop.

📱 It works in the browser on your phone and your computer, with nothing to download. You can also install it as an app: on Android, tap Install; on iPhone, in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

🎥 During the video chat

  • 💬 Chat with automatic translation: write in your language and the other person reads it in theirs. Use Translate / Show original to switch.
  • 👍 ❤️ Reactions: they light up on both screens and add up on the other person’s profile.
  • 🤝 Add friend to talk to that person again another day.
  • 🔄 Switch camera (front or back) without cutting the call.
  • 🖥️ View options: make the other person’s video bigger, swap boxes, use wide view or go full screen.
  • 💡 Conversation starters: above the chat I suggest a question. Tap it to send it, or 🔀 for another one.
  • 🌡️ Chemistry meter: a bar under the video that heats up as you talk. At 100° you both get +5 chilis.
  • 🤔 After 20 seconds or more, we ask you privately: “Would you talk to that person again?” Yes or no — nobody else sees your answer.

🔥 Spicy mode

Optional and off by default. It unlocks the spicy levels of the games and the compliments, and it only works if both of you have it on. You can turn it on or off whenever you like.

🎲 Games

They appear in a strip below the video, so they never cover the person you’re talking to:

  • Truth or dare
  • Would you rather?
  • Rock, paper, scissors
  • Draw & guess

Inviting someone to play needs Premium; anyone can accept. All the details are in Games and powers.

🌶️ Chilis

Chilis are the site’s points: they’re free and can’t be bought. Here’s how you earn them:

  • Daily login: 5 per day of streak (up to 30).
  • Each game played: +2.
  • Receiving a ❤️: +1. Receiving a compliment: +2.
  • A conversation longer than 1 minute: +1.
  • Each correct guess in Draw & guess: +2.
  • Chemistry explosion: +5.
  • Weekly challenges: +50.

You can earn up to 120 a day. And there’s more:

  • 🔥 Hot hour: every day from 22:00 to 23:00 (Spanish time) the chilis you earn count double (×2).
  • 📅 Streak: come back every day and your daily reward grows.
  • 🎯 3 weekly challenges and 9 badges to collect.
  • 🏆 Weekly ranking of who earned the most chilis. Prefer not to appear? Tick “Don’t show me in the ranking”.

⚡ Superpowers

Spend your chilis on superpowers:

🪙 Tips

They only appear if the site has them switched on, so you may not see them yet.

  • 🪙 Send a tip: during a video chat tap the 🪙 button, pick 10, 25, 50 or 100 coins (or another amount) and send it. It’s a voluntary gift and can’t be refunded.
  • 👛 Wallet: in “My account → Wallet” you buy coins in one-time packs, see what you’ve earned (held, available, being withdrawn and paid out) and choose whether you accept tips.
  • 💶 Earnings and withdrawals: for each coin you receive you earn its value minus the site’s fee. It’s held for a few days for security; then, above a minimum and after verifying your identity with Veriff, you can ask us to send it to you by bank transfer.
  • 🚫 Never pay or ask for tips in exchange for someone doing something on camera: it’s forbidden. If someone asks you, report it.
