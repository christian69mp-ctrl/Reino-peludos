Cancellation and refunds

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This is a translation of the Spanish original. If there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

Draft pending legal review.

1. Plans and prices

  • Weekly: €7.99 every 7 days.
  • Monthly: €19.99 every 30 days.

Taxes are included unless stated otherwise. You can pay by card, Google Pay or Apple Pay (a subscription that renews) or with cryptocurrency (a one-time payment). Payments are processed by Segpay. You can find what Premium includes in the Terms of use.

2. How renewals work

  • Card, Google Pay and Apple Pay: the subscription renews automatically at the end of each period (7 or 30 days) at the same price, until you cancel it.
  • Cryptocurrency: a one-time payment that gives you 7 or 30 days of Premium, depending on the plan. It does not renew: when the period ends, Premium ends, and you can pay again if you want.

3. How to cancel

You can cancel at any time, in either of these ways:

  • On the website: My account → Premium → Manage.
  • Directly with Segpay, at cs.segpay.com.

After you cancel, you will not be charged again and you keep Premium until the end of the period you have already paid for. If you paid with cryptocurrency, there is nothing to cancel. Deleting your account does not cancel a card subscription on its own: cancel it with Segpay as well so that you are not charged again.

4. Right of withdrawal

As a consumer in the European Union, you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a contract made online. Because Premium is activated instantly, at checkout you expressly ask for it to start immediately and acknowledge that you lose the right of withdrawal once it is activated.

5. When we do give refunds

  • Duplicate charge: you were charged twice for the same period.
  • Unauthorised charge: a charge that you did not make or authorise.
  • Technical failure on our side: a problem of ours prevents you from using Premium and we do not fix it within 72 hours.

6. How to request a refund

  • Write to us within 30 days of the charge, through the contact page or at soporte@laruletacaliente.com.
  • Include the email of your account and the date and amount of the charge, and tell us briefly what happened.
  • We will reply within 7 days.
  • If the refund is approved, it is made to the same payment method through Segpay. Depending on your bank, it may take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

7. Cryptocurrency payments

Payments made with cryptocurrency are only refunded where the law requires it.

8. When there is no refund

  • If your account is banned for breaking the Community rules, the amount paid is not refunded.
  • If you open an unjustified chargeback with your bank, we will close the account. If something is wrong, contact us first so we can look into it.

9. Launch period

During the launch period, Premium may be free for a while. We will let you know before we start charging; nothing will be charged without your express consent.

10. How the charge appears

The charge appears on your bank or card statement as “Segpay”. If you see a charge you do not recognise, contact us.
