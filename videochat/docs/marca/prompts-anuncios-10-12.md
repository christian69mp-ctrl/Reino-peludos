## Reglas para que no te tumben el anuncio

- **Todas las personas, claramente adultas (25-35 años)**, vestidas normal, nada sexual ni insinuante.
- **No es un testimonio:** nadie dice «yo lo probé y conocí a…». La chica del 10 **explica cómo funciona** (todo lo
  que dice es verdad). Inventar opiniones de clientes con personas falsas es publicidad engañosa.
- **Las pantallas se ven de lado, de espaldas o desenfocadas**, para que la IA no escriba letras raras.
- **Marca el vídeo como «creado con IA»** al publicar: en TikTok es obligatorio con personas realistas
  (opción «Contenido generado por IA» al subir) e Instagram puede añadir la etiqueta «Info de IA» solo.

**Prompt negativo (Kling):**
```
text, letters, words, numbers, subtitles, captions, logo, watermark, brand names, nudity, sexual content, minors, teenagers, deformed hands, extra fingers, distorted faces, blurry, low quality
```

## Anuncio 10 · «Cómo conocer gente sin descargar nada» (UGC / selfie)

```
Vertical 9:16 smartphone selfie video, authentic UGC TikTok style, handheld with slight natural shake, 8 seconds. A friendly Spanish woman in her late twenties with wavy dark hair, wearing a casual oversized hoodie, sits cross-legged on her bed in a cozy bedroom at night lit by warm fairy lights and a pink LED strip. Opening frame: she leans very close to the camera with raised eyebrows and a cheeky half-smile, as if sharing a secret, and immediately starts talking. She then holds up a second phone toward the camera with its screen turned away, mimes tapping one button, and her face lights up with a surprised, genuine laugh. She ends looking straight into the lens, nodding confidently and giving a small wink. Natural skin texture, realistic lighting, shallow depth of field, no visible text or logos anywhere. Keep the top third of the frame mostly free (ceiling and fairy lights) in the first 3 seconds.
Audio: she speaks Spanish from Spain, natural, warm, fast and enthusiastic, perfectly lip-synced: "¿Conocer gente nueva sin descargarte nada ni registrarte? Mira: entras, pulsas Empezar… y ya estás hablando con alguien. ¡Y el chat se traduce solo!" Soft trending lo-fi pop beat underneath.
```

## Anuncio 11 · «Madrid llama a México» (cinematográfico)

```
Vertical 9:16 cinematic video, 8 seconds, shot like a modern music video, anamorphic look, rich colors, night. Opening frame: extreme close-up of a man's thumb pressing a glowing round pink-to-orange button on a phone screen, with a burst of light filling the frame and a deep bass hit. Cut: a man in his late twenties in a small Madrid apartment at night, city lights and rooftops through the window, sitting on the floor against the sofa, holding the phone; his bored face turns into a surprised smile. Cut: a woman in her late twenties in a colorful Mexico City apartment with plants and warm lamps, laughing at her laptop, covering her mouth with her hand. Fast match cuts between both on the beat: they wave at each other through their screens (screens seen from the side, blurred), play rock-paper-scissors with their hands toward the camera at the same time, both laugh. Final shot: split vertical composition, Madrid on top and Mexico City below, both looking at their screens smiling, warm pink and orange glow. Both fully dressed in casual clothes, friendly and fun, not romantic or sexual. No visible text or logos. Keep the top third of the frame darker and clear in the first 3 seconds.
Audio: catchy upbeat Latin electronic pop with a strong drop after the button press, a soft notification ding when they connect, playful laughter. No voice-over.
```
